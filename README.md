### What I actually do

Most early-stage tech companies don't have a growth problem.  
They have five people who each own a piece of it and nobody who owns the whole.  
Sales blames marketing. Marketing blames product. The founder blames the market.  
Meanwhile the people who already paid quietly leave, and nobody owns that.

I find those gaps:

→ map where the money leaks — acquisition, onboarding, retention, pricing  
→ fix the biggest leak first  
→ draw a 90-day plan for the rest  
→ automate the repetitive 80% with AI  
→ hand it to your team, so you're not stuck with me
